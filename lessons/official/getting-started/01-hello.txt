# Welcome to typelab

This is your first lesson. Type every character exactly as it appears.

Characters you type correctly show up green.
Characters you mistype show up red.
Use backspace to fix a mistake.

## Controls

Press enter to skip the rest of a line.
Press ctrl + d to skip this whole section and jump to the next heading.
Press ctrl + a to jump back to the previous heading.
Press ctrl + c to leave the lesson and return to the lesson picker.

## Practice

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
Typing by hand builds muscle memory faster than reading alone.
